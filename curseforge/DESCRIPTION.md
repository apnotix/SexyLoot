# SexyLoot

![WoW Forever](https://img.shields.io/badge/WoW-Forever-e8c26a?style=for-the-badge) ![WoW Retail](https://img.shields.io/badge/WoW-Retail-c8a24a?style=for-the-badge) ![Interface](https://img.shields.io/badge/Interface-120100%20%7C%2016001-3b2b12?style=for-the-badge)

**Loot, rolls and your bags - in one clean, consistent look.**

SexyLoot replaces the loot window and the group-loot roll frames, shows every player's roll right on the item, keeps a group loot feed and tidies up your bags. Everything is placed and tuned in **Edit Mode**.

Works on **World of Warcraft: Forever** and **Retail**. Styled like the other Sexy addons: dark brown panels, gold frame, title bands with badges and colored labels.

## Features

- 🎲 **Roll window** - one card per item with Need, Greed and Pass, each player's choice and result right on it. After you rolled, the item link and tooltip still work.
- 🏆 **Winner display** - a big card with a glow when you win, a slim toast when someone else does.
- 🎁 **Loot window** - your own slot list with a take-all button and quality highlights.
- 📜 **Group feed** - who rolled what and who got which item.
- 🎯 **Free rolls** - `/roll` results sorted highest first, for chests and other manual rolls.
- 🧲 **Loot popup** - recently looted items and money (summed or one by one), with value.
- 🎒 **My loot** - a session list with total value and value per hour.
- 💰 **Wallet** - your gold and the sell value of your bags, plus all your characters in the tooltip.
- 🧹 **Bag clean-up** - warns when your bags are almost full. A dialog lists your cheapest items to destroy (two clicks, never quest items) and opens by itself next to the loot window when the loot does not fit.
- ✨ **Disenchanting** - a window with every disenchantable weapon and armor piece, one click casts the spell. An optional button opens it, shown only if you know Disenchant.
- 🏷️ **Real prices** - values come from **Auctionator**, **Mastercraft**, **TSM**, **Oribos Exchange** or **Auctioneer** if installed (vendor price shown below), otherwise the vendor price.

## Edit Mode

Click any SexyLoot window in Edit Mode: the dialog has collapsible sections, sample data shows what you are editing and buttons link related windows. Every window can be moved, scaled, hidden, hidden in combat or shown on mouseover, and docked to another window.

## Commands

| Command | Description |
|---|---|
| `/sl test` | Fill all windows with sample data |
| `/sl bags` | Open the bag clean-up dialog |
| `/sl de` | Open the disenchant window |
| `/sl price [item]` | Show what each price addon reports for an item |

Long form: `/sexyloot`

## 🇩🇪 Deutsch

**SexyLoot** ersetzt Beutefenster und Würfelfenster, zeigt jeden Wurf direkt am Item, führt einen Gruppen-Feed und räumt deine Taschen auf.

- Würfelfenster, Gewinner-Anzeige, Beutefenster, Gruppen-Feed, freie Würfe, Loot-Einblendung mit Geld, „Meine Beute“ und Geld-Anzeige
- Taschen aufräumen (Hinweis bei fast vollem Inventar) und Entzauber-Fenster mit Knopf
- Preise von Auctionator, Mastercraft, TSM, Oribos Exchange oder Auctioneer, sonst Händlerpreis
- Alles im Edit Mode einstellbar, funktioniert auf Deutsch und Englisch
